# Audit policy fields

Full inventory every day. Supported changes are reported when the agent observes them.

| Field | Type | Unit | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| `sparklogs.data.audit_policy.audit_framework` | string |  | Which audit system the policy belongs to. `windows_advanced_audit` is the Windows Advanced Audit Policy subcategories, the only one reported today. Reserved for other platforms: `auditd` (Linux audit daemon rules), `openbsm` (macOS BSM audit classes) and `none` (no audit system). Always present. |
| `sparklogs.data.audit_policy.audit_events` | string_array |  | Which events the audit policy records, sorted. Each entry is `<family>[_<detail>]_<outcome>`, outcome `success` or `failure`. A family entry (`logon_success`) is present whenever any of its subcategories records that outcome, beside each such subcategory's own entry (`logon_logon_success`). Families: `logon`, `authentication`, `process_creation`, `process_termination`, `detailed_tracking`, `file_access`, `object_access`, `network`, `account_management`, `policy_change`, `privilege_use`, `system`, `directory_service`. An empty array is a policy that was read and records nothing. Absent when the policy could not be read, preserving the previous result. Under `auditd` the entries will be the rule keys, with no outcome suffix; under `openbsm` the classes map to families: `lo` logon, `aa` authentication, `ex` process_creation, `pc` process_termination, `fr`/`fw`/`fa`/`fm`/`fc`/`fd` file_access_read/write/attribute_read/attribute_modify/create/delete, `ad` account_management, policy_change, privilege_use and system, `nt` network, `ot` object_access. |
| `sparklogs.data.audit_policy.audit_subcategories` | object_array |  | Every audit subcategory the OS defines, in the order `auditpol` lists them, category by category. Each entry has `native_id`, the subcategory GUID in lowercase textual form, and `success` and `failure`, whether the policy records that outcome. A subcategory this agent maps also carries `audit_event` (its `audit_events` entry without the `_success` or `_failure` outcome) and `family`; its `native_name` and `native_category` are the English names `auditpol` prints, not localized, and it carries no `native_name_source`. One it does not map carries no `audit_event` or `family`; its `native_name` and `native_category` come from the OS in the host's display language, with `native_name_source` `os`; each name is absent when the OS has none for it, and `native_name_source` stays. The three `IPsec` mode subcategories are three entries sharing one `audit_event`. The `native_*` members are the audit framework's own: under `auditd` and `openbsm` they will carry that framework's identifiers and names. Describes the policy and is never compared: `audit_events` alone decides a change. Absent when the policy could not be read, preserving the previous result. |
